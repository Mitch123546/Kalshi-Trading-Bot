03 · The learning model

CHALLENGER, NOT ORACLE

A second brain is useful.
A second unchecked bot is not.

Train a separate research model on years of 15-minute gold bars, then challenge it with Kalshi-specific data as that history accumulates. It should produce probabilities—not orders—and earn promotion through untouched forward tests.

GOOD IDEA

Use years of underlying gold data

It gives the model many market regimes and enough observations to study session effects, volatility clustering, and short-term momentum or mean reversion.

WATCH OUT

Gold bars are not Kalshi fills

Underlying history can train direction. It cannot recreate Kalshi's historical spread, queue, fees, target construction, or whether your order would have filled.

BAD IDEA

Let two bots trade independently

They can double the same exposure, disagree without a tie-break rule, and learn from overlapping data. One execution and risk engine should arbitrate every model.

04 · The only scoreboard that matters

05 · Live safety

Production is a
three-key vault.

Possessing an API key is deliberately not enough to place a live order.

KEY 01KALSHI_ENV=prod
KEY 02KALSHI_ALLOW_LIVE=true
KEY 03--confirm-live

All three must be present in the same run. Paper mode is the default.

Every cycle can say no.
